# VibeCraft - User Guide

## 🎵 Welcome to VibeCraft!

VibeCraft is your personal music space where you can:
- Import songs from Spotify playlists
- Add YouTube videos
- Upload your own audio files
- Record voice memos
- Create custom playlists
- Share your music page with friends

---

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### **Import from Spotify (Recommended Method)**

**Step 1: Go to Spotify**
- Open [open.spotify.com](https://open.spotify.com)
- Navigate to your favorite playlist

**Step 2: Select Your Songs**
- Click on the first song in your playlist
- Hold Shift and click the last song (selects all songs)
- Or press Ctrl+A to select all songs
- Right-click and Copy (or Ctrl+C)

**Step 3: Import to VibeCraft**
- Go to VibeCraft
- Click "Add Music" button
- Find "Import from Spotify" section
- Click "Paste Spotify song list"
- Paste your songs (Ctrl+V)
- Click "Import Songs"

**That's it!** Your entire playlist will be imported automatically.

---

## 🎬 Adding YouTube Videos

**Step 1: Find Your Video**
- Go to YouTube
- Find the video you want to add
- Copy the video URL

**Step 2: Add to VibeCraft**
- Go to VibeCraft
- Click "Add Music"
- Paste the YouTube URL in the input field
- Click "Add"

---

## 📤 Uploading Your Own Files

**Step 1: Prepare Your Files**
- MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A audio files
- MP4, WEBM video files

**Step 2: Upload**
- Go to VibeCraft
- Click "Add Music"
- Click or drag files to the upload zone
- Files will be added to your playlist

---

## 🎤 Recording Voice Memos

**Step 1: Start Recording**
- Go to VibeCraft
- Find the "Voice Recorder" section
- Click the red record button

**Step 2: Stop & Save**
- Click the red button again to stop
- Name your recording
- Click "Save to Playlist"

---

## 🎵 Managing Your Playlist

**View Your Songs:**
- Click the "Songs" button in the top right
- See all your imported songs

**Play Music:**
- Click any song to play it
- Use playback controls to pause, skip, etc.

**Remove Songs:**
- Hover over a song
- Click the delete button

**Change Order:**
- Drag and drop songs to reorder them

---

## 🎨 Customizing Your Space

**Change Background:**
- Click the background area
- Choose from preset backgrounds
- Or upload your own image

**Change Accent Color:**
- Click "Settings"
- Choose your preferred color

---

## 📤 Sharing Your Music Page

**Share Your Link:**
- Your page has a unique share link
- Copy the share URL
- Send it to friends

**Shared Pages:**
- Create custom pages with specific songs
- Share those pages with different people

---

## 🎯 Tips for Best Results

**Spotify Import:**
- Works best with public playlists
- Select all songs at once for fastest import
- Artist names help find better YouTube matches

**YouTube Search:**
- Includes "official" in search for better results
- Song + Artist combination works best
- Avoid remix versions if you want originals

**File Uploads:**
- Use MP3 for best compatibility
- Keep files under 10MB for faster loading
- Name your files clearly for better organization

---

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Songs Not Found:**
- Try different title/artist combinations
- Some songs may not be on YouTube
- Use the original song title

**Audio Not Playing:**
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Some videos may be region-locked

**Import Failed:**
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure VibeCraft server is running
- Try pasting songs in smaller batches
